How Dialectic works

Not an assistant in a chat window. Three participants reasoning on one record.

The three voices

│Human You and whoever else is in the room. Peers, not users. ◇Claude A participant that challenges and synthesises. It is not here to help you. ✕Provoker Claude in adversarial register, called in when the room is agreeing too easily.

Speech acts

¶Text Ordinary contribution. Nothing asserted, nothing fixed. ◆Claim An assertion you are willing to defend. Marking it invites challenge. ?Question An open question put to the room. Raises the odds Claude speaks. ≡Definition Fixes the meaning of a term for this room. Load-bearing; everything after leans on it.

Summoning and interrupting

@llm Summon Claude directly. It answers the room, not just you. Stop Cut Claude off mid-thought. The partial response is kept on the record, marked cancelled. Auto With auto interjection on, Claude decides for itself when to speak. Tune it in room settings.

Structure

Fork Branch from any message into a new thread. The original line is untouched. Reply Anchor a message to an earlier one. The reference stays clickable. Memory Shared and versioned. Both humans and Claude read it; write to it to change how Claude reasons.
